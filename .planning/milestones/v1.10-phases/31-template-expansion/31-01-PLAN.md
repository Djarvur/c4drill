<objective>
Land the BC-1 parser prerequisite (D-08): teach the parser to recognize three reserved top-level tables (`[template.*]`, `[[use]]`, `[[include]]`) so they neither create phantom units nor leak into the unit loop, and route `template`/`use` into new `Model.Templates`/`Model.Instantiations` fields consumed by Plan 02's expansion pass.

Purpose: This is the foundation Plan 02 (and Phase 32's include feature) depend on. Without it, `[template.microservice]` registers `microservice` as a subunit of a phantom parent `template`. It is parser-only — no expansion logic, no substitution, no deep-copy. Fully testable in isolation.

Output: Modified `internal/parser/parser.go` (skip rule + extraction + Model fields + TemplateDef/Instantiation types), extended `internal/parser/parser_test.go`, three new testdata fixtures.
</objective>
